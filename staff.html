Our Staff

The dedicated team that makes Saint Joseph Catholic School a center of excellence

The Heartbeat of Our School

The staff members of Saint Joseph Catholic School are considered the backbone of our institution. Their dedication, passion, and commitment to excellence create a nurturing environment where students thrive academically, morally, and spiritually. Every day, our teachers, administrators, and support staff work tirelessly to shape the future leaders of Ethiopia.

Message from the Principal

Dr. Belayneh Medanit

School Principal

Welcome to St. Joseph Catholic School, Adama. Since our inception in the 1950s, St. Joseph has served as a cornerstone of excellence and a beacon of hope for the youth of Adama. As a Lasallian institution, we are driven by the foundational mission of St. John Baptist de La Salle to "teach minds and touch hearts," ensuring that every student—regardless of their background—receives a transformative education that empowers both their intellect and their character.

Our vision is to remain a preeminent academic center, deeply centered on Faith in God and the rich heritage of Catholic and Lasallian traditions. At St. Joseph, students are both challenged by a rigorous, world-class curriculum and formed through a robust framework of Lasallian ethics. We pride ourselves on being an inclusive community where all faiths are welcomed and celebrated, fostering a profound sense of brotherhood and sisterhood among our students.

Today, we serve nearly 3,000 students with a strategy that looks firmly toward the future. We are committed to tapping into the unique talents of each student, providing the environment necessary to propel them toward excellence. With a student body that is 60% female, we place a strategic priority on leadership and capacity building, ensuring our graduates are prepared to impact a globalized world with competence and integrity.

In collaboration with our Brothers, Alumni, and Parents, we invite you to join us in this journey. Together, we continue transforming lives through holistic, joyful, and forward-thinking education.

We are St. Joseph's; we are Lasallian.

In Faith and Service,
Brother Belayneh Medhanit, FSC, Ph.D.
Principal

Message from the Vice Principal

Brother Solomon Tobias

Vice Principal

St. Joseph Catholic School Adama stands proudly as a Lasallian Catholic institution dedicated to forming young people not only in academic excellence but also in faith, service, and character. Rooted in the rich tradition of Lasallian education, the school embraces core values such as faith in the presence of God, respect for all persons, quality education, inclusive community, and concern for the poor and social justice.

At St. Joseph, each day begins with the Word of God and prayer. This daily practice grounds the entire school community in faith, reminding students and staff of God's presence in their lives. It sets a tone of reflection, gratitude, and purpose, helping learners to approach their studies and relationships with humility, discipline, and a sense of higher calling.

Learning goes beyond the classroom. Students are actively engaged in service learning programs that connect their studies with real-life experiences of compassion and responsibility. Through regular outreach initiatives, they participate in charitable activities, supporting those in need and developing a strong sense of empathy and social awareness.

The school community takes pride in nurturing well-disciplined and respectful students who demonstrate integrity, cooperation, and leadership. With a commitment to holistic education, St. Joseph Catholic School Adama continues to shape young individuals who are not only academically capable but also socially responsible and deeply committed to making a difference in their communities.

In Faith and Service,
Brother Solomon Tobias
Vice Principal
